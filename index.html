01 — How it works

Three steps to
game night.

01

Create a Room

One person opens The Clubhouse and hits create. They get a six-digit code — no sign-up, no faff.

02

Share the Code

Drop it in the group chat. Everyone joins from their phone, laptop, or tablet — wherever they are in the world.

03

Pick a Game & Play

The host picks a game and every screen syncs instantly. Same moment, same chaos, completely live.

02 — The Lineup

Six ways to
cause a scene.

?
Classic
Trivia Night

Host picks a pack, questions hit every screen at once. Answer fast, score higher. Chaos is guaranteed.

Chaotic
PowerPoint Party

Everyone submits a 5-slide deck on a random topic. The host plays them. The group judges. Tears may happen.

Dramatic
Murder Mystery

Roles are assigned. Clues drop. Accusations fly. One of you did it — and nobody can be trusted.

Opinionated
Carpet Call

Rate red carpet looks in real time. Serve, Mid, or Crime Scene — everyone votes before the reveal.

Unhinged
Party Games

Truth or dare, hot takes, most likely to — prompt-based chaos that works for every group dynamic.

Strategic
Board Games

Digital adaptations of your favourites. Wordplay, bluffing, strategy — the friendships will be tested.
